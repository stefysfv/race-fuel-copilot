Terms of Use & Not Medical Advice

Not medical advice: Race Fuel Co-Pilot provides planning estimates for carbohydrate, fluid, and sodium intake based on general trail-running heuristics. It is not medical, nutritional, or clinical advice, and it does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any medical condition (including hyponatremia, dehydration, heat illness, or gastrointestinal distress).

1. Test in training & listen to your body

Every athlete's gut tolerance, sweat rate, and medical history are different. Test any fueling strategy in training before race day, drink to thirst, and follow race medical staff instructions over any app cue.

2. Navigation & GPS limitations

Phone GPS can drift in mountains, forests, and canyons. Do not rely on this app as your sole navigation or safety device. Always carry required race gear and follow official course markings.
