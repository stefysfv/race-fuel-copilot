Privacy Policy

Short version: Your GPX files, athlete profile, GPS location, and fueling plans stay on your phone. Your location is read when you tap Sync GPS or GI Rescue. There are no accounts, no cloud database, no analytics, and no automatic error reporting.

1. On-device computation

All route parsing, Grade Adjusted Pace math, fueling schedules, and Sync GPS recalculations run locally inside your browser using WebAssembly (Pyodide). Your GPX uploads and GPS coordinates are never uploaded to a server.

2. Location: check-ins only

The app is a check-in guide. Your phone's location is read only when you tap Sync GPS or GI Rescue, one reading per tap, and your browser asks for permission first. The app does not track you in the background, does not watch your location while the screen is locked, does not keep the screen awake, and does not send notifications. The reading is used on your phone to snap you to the course and recalculate your plan. The last snapped position is saved on your phone with your plan and is never uploaded.

3. Local storage on your device

The app stores your preferences, active plan, cached offline files, and optional post-race notes in your browser's localStorage, IndexedDB, and Cache Storage so the app works without signal on race day. Clearing site data in your browser removes everything.

4. Optional feedback & bug reports

Post-race notes ("How did it go?") remain on your phone unless you explicitly tap Send feedback, which opens your own email client or chosen form with only the fields shown in the preview (never your GPX track). Diagnostics are strictly opt-in via Copy diagnostics—nothing is ever sent automatically.

5. Third-party map tiles

When online map tiles are enabled, your browser requests the visible map area from Esri. If a tile fails to load, the app can switch to OpenStreetMap. These requests disclose your IP address and the area displayed to the provider. The fueling plan and GPX file are processed on your device.
