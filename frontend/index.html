How fueling zones work

Steep climbs pull blood away from your stomach and steep descents slosh it. The planner slices your GPX into 500 m segments and shifts feeds within ±6 minutes onto safer ground:

  • Green (−8% to +5%): Runnable ground — full carb dose (gels or real food).
  • Yellow (+5% to +10% or −8% to −12%): Moderate slope — half dose as gel or drink mix.
  • Red (steeper than +10% or −12%): Steep climb or drop — 15 g liquid micro-dose only, never carried as debt.
