Minetti calibration — recorded moving time vs engine relative cost
Engine constants were not changed.

Method
  Slices: engine prepare_course + slice_segments (about 500 m, net grade).
  Excluded from moving time: speed under 1 km/h, aid dwells, non-positive timestamps,
  and isolated jumps (a stretch of at most 80 m peaking at 30 km/h or faster).
  Aid dwell: within 150 m of a catalogued station, inside a 20 m band, for at least 45 s, slower than 4 km/h.
  Left out of the pool: a recording with at least 0.5 km sustained at or above 25 km/h in stretches of 200 m.
  Flat pace: each recording's own moving pace on slices graded -5% to +5%.
  Prediction: moving km x that flat pace x gap_cost_factor(slice grade).
  Error: predicted minutes minus actual moving minutes. Negative means the model is too fast.
  Unreliable: fewer than 4 slices, or under 1 km moving, or under 3 min moving.
